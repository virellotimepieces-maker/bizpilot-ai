# Bizlyro outreach playbook

Internal notes for direct outreach. This file is not a mailer. Send each message yourself, to a business you have a reason to contact. Do not scrape lists or send bulk email.

Public product: Bizlyro AI at https://bizlyro.com
Price: $29.99 per month after the owner creates an account and subscribes.
The local demo at https://bizlyro.com/demo is sample data in the browser. It does not call Stripe or a paid AI model.

## Ideal customer profile

A fit is an owner-operated local business with:

- A website where one chat widget can be installed
- A person who will review leads. Bizlyro does not close the job or send the email
- Hours, a service area, or other facts they are willing to publish
- Prices or policies they want repeated, and a willingness to leave unpublished numbers unanswered
- Missed questions while they are on a job, with a patient, or away from the desk

The first three outreach fits are contractors and home services, dental clinics, and other local service businesses.

A poor fit wants the assistant to invent a quote, confirm a time that was never opened, give dental or medical advice, or send email on its own.

## Prospect qualification checklist

Use this before a demo. A "no" on the first four is a reason to wait.

- They have a website, or a page they control, where a snippet can be added.
- An owner or front desk will read leads. There is no overnight staff requirement.
- They can name the facts that are safe to repeat: hours, neighborhoods, and any published fee.
- They accept that an unpublished price, policy, availability, or promotion is not guessed.
- $29.99 per month is a price they can consider. There is no free paid plan.
- They do not need Shopify, WooCommerce, or calendar booking to see the product. Those are not part of this pitch.

Signup creates the account, then the owner subscribes on the billing page. The widget and the paid Leads desk come after that subscription. The demo is the thing to show before asking them to pay.

## Tracking

Bizlyro workspace analytics count stored workspace records such as conversations and leads. They do not read marketing query parameters.

These parameters are safe to put on a link you send, and the pages still load:

- `utm_source`
- `utm_medium`
- `utm_campaign`

Bizlyro does not record them. There is no campaign report in the product, and this playbook does not add one. Keep your own note of who you contacted and which link you used.

Example, for your own notes only:

`https://bizlyro.com/ai-chatbot-for-contractors?utm_source=outreach&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=contractors`

The matching demo ignores the UTM parameters and still opens the sample:

`https://bizlyro.com/demo?preset=service-lumen&utm_source=outreach&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=contractors`

## 60-second personalized demo-video script

Record the screen yourself. Use their website for the first line, then the matching sample demo. Say that the sample is demo data.

1. 0–10 seconds. "This is [their business]. The question I keep seeing is [one real question from their site, such as hours or service area]."
2. 10–25 seconds. Open the matching sample. Read the visitor question. Show the answer and point at the published fact it used. "Bizlyro only repeats what the business has written down. This sample is not your live site."
3. 25–40 seconds. Show the second question, the one that asks for an unpublished price or promotion. "It does not invent that number. Honest AI does not invent prices, policies, or business information."
4. 40–50 seconds. Show the sample lead: name, email, and request. "You see the lead. Bizlyro does not email them for you."
5. 50–60 seconds. "It can do this 24/7 from the facts you publish. Bizlyro AI is $29.99 per month after you create an account. This is the page to start: https://bizlyro.com/signup"

Do not add a customer count, a star rating, a countdown, or a result you have not measured.
