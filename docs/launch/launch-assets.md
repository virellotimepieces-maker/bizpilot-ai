## Long product description

Bizlyro AI is a website assistant for an owner who is also the front desk. Customers still ask about hours, service areas, and published prices after the business is closed or while the owner is with someone else. A contact form stores a name. It does not answer the question.

You publish the facts that are safe to repeat: hours, services, prices you are willing to quote, policies, and the cases that need a person. One widget on your existing site can answer those published questions 24/7. When a visitor leaves contact details, or asks for a quote or a callback, the lead waits in your workspace. You review it. Bizlyro does not email the visitor for you, and it does not post to a social network for you.

Honest AI never invents unknown prices, policies, promotions, or business facts. If the fact is not in the knowledge base, the assistant should say so. Dental and medical questions are not answered as advice. An appointment request is a request, not a reserved time.

The plan is $29.99 per month for one workspace, one website widget, and 500 AI-generated customer replies in a billing month. Cancel anytime. There is no automatic overage charge and no free paid plan. Get started creates an account, then you subscribe. The local demo is sample data in the browser. It does not call Stripe.

## Founder / product story

Bizlyro was made for the gap after the owner leaves the desk. The website keeps receiving the same questions — are you open, do you cover this street, what does this published service cost — and the only safe answer is the one the business already wrote down. The product is that boundary: answer the published fact, keep the lead, and refuse to invent the rest. This story does not include a customer count, an award, or a press mention.

## Target customers

Owner-operated local businesses with a website and a person who will read leads. The clearest fits are contractors and home services, dental clinics, and other local service businesses. A poor fit wants invented quotes, clinical advice, or email that sends itself.
